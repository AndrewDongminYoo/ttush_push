Push smart. Hold your ground. Win two rounds.

Ttush Push is a turn-based tactics game where every move can change the board. Guide the Azure Expedition or Ember Expedition across floating ruins, push rival explorers out of position, and protect the footholds beneath your own team.

CHOOSE YOUR RIVAL
Share one device for a two-player match, or face four AI difficulty levels from Easy to Expert.

READ THE BOARD
A filled marker shows a move. A ring shows a Push. Damaged footholds collapse when an explorer leaves them again, so every route can become a trap.

WIN YOUR WAY
Take a round by knocking out the rival expedition or leaving it with no legal move. Win two rounds to claim the match.

LEARN AS YOU PLAY
A first-match guide explains selection, movement, pushing, and crumbling footholds without interrupting the strategy.

Ttush Push plays offline. It has no ads, no account, and no online multiplayer. Your coach progress stays on your device.
